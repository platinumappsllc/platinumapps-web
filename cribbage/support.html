Cribbage Platinum support

We read every message, and usually answer within a couple of days.

Reach us

Write to platinumapps.support@gmail.com.

The quickest way is from inside the game: Menu > Settings > Send feedback, or Report this hand on the deck screen after a hand. Either starts an email with the game attached, so we can look at the very cards you saw. Nothing is sent until you send it.

Common questions

Is the deal really random?

Yes. Each game is one shuffle for both players, and the difficulty setting changes only how well the computer plays, never the cards. After each hand, tap Deck to see every card in the order it was dealt. Menu > Stats shows your average hand and crib beside the computer's.

I think a hand was scored wrong.

Tap Deck when the hand has been counted, then Report this hand. We can replay that exact hand and will tell you what we find.

How do I remove the ads?

Menu > Settings > Ads > Remove ads. It is a single purchase, not a subscription, and it applies on every device signed in to your Apple Account.

I bought Remove ads and still see the banner, or I have a new phone.

Open Menu > Settings > Ads and tap Restore purchase. Make sure the device is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. If that does not work, write to us.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I play against another person?

No. Cribbage Platinum is one player against the computer.

How do I learn the game?

Menu > How to play has the rules, and Play the lesson at the top walks you through one hand with a coach.
