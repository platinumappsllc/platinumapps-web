Cribbage Platinum privacy policy

Effective date: to be set when published. Draft for review.

Cribbage Platinum is a cribbage game for iPhone and iPad from Platinum Apps LLC ("we"). This page says what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • You do not need an account, and the app never asks for your name, email address or contacts.
  • Your games, statistics and settings stay on your device.
  • The app shows one banner advertisement, after you have finished your first game. The companies that supply the ads receive some information about your device in order to show and measure them.
  • You are asked before the app or its advertising partners may track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can say no and the game works the same.
  • One purchase removes the ads. Once you have bought it, the advertising software is not started at all.
  • If the app crashes or freezes, a technical report is sent to us so that we can fix it. It does not say who you are.
  • If you choose to send us feedback, you send an email, and we see what any email shows us.

What stays on your device

The game in progress, your lifetime statistics, your settings and the fact that you have removed the ads are stored on your device only. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

The app shows a single banner at the bottom of the table. There are no full-screen, video or rewarded ads. No ad is shown, and no advertising software is started, until you have finished your first game.

The banner is supplied through AppLovin MAX, which passes each ad request to these advertising partners. Each acts under its own privacy policy:

  • AppLovin: legal.applovin.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • InMobi: inmobi.com/privacy-policy
  • Liftoff (Vungle): liftoff.ai/privacy-policy
  • MobileFuse: mobilefuse.com/privacy-policy
  • Verve: verve.com/privacy-policy
  • Smaato: smaato.com/privacy

What the advertising partners receive

To show an ad, to count that it was shown or tapped, to limit how often you see the same one and to detect fraud, the advertising software sends its makers:

  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you have allowed tracking (see below); otherwise an identifier that is specific to this app's developer and cannot follow you into other companies' apps;
  • your internet address, from which an approximate location (country, region or city) can be worked out. The app never asks for your precise location;
  • your device model, version of iOS, language, time zone, screen size and network type;
  • the name and version of this app;
  • which ads were shown and whether you tapped them;
  • technical information about how the advertising software is working.

The advertising partners do not receive your name, email address, contacts, photos, or anything about your games. We do not receive from them any information that identifies you.

Tracking and personalised ads

The first time ads are about to be shown, your device asks whether the app may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, the advertising partners may use your device's advertising identifier to show ads based on your interests and to measure their results across apps. If you do not, they may not, and you will see ads that are not based on your activity elsewhere.

You can change your answer at any time in the Settings app: Privacy & Security > Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are also asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice later from the app's settings.

Removing the ads

A one-time purchase in Settings > Ads removes the banner. Once you have bought it, the advertising software is not started and nothing is sent to the advertising partners. The purchase is handled by Apple; we never see your payment details, and the app only remembers on your device that the ads were removed.

What is sent to us

Crash and performance reports

When the app crashes, freezes, or is closed by the system, it sends a report to Sentry, a service we use to find and fix faults. Reports are also sent in summary when the app is opened and closed, so that we can tell how often a version crashes.

A report contains:

  • what went wrong and where in the app's code;
  • the app's version, your device model and your version of iOS;
  • where the game stood: the number that produced the shuffle, the hand number, the stage of the hand, the score, and your difficulty, counting and speed settings;
  • a random number created when the app is installed, so that we can tell that two reports came from the same copy of the app. It is not your device's advertising identifier and is not connected to your name, Apple Account or anything else about you. It changes if you delete and reinstall the app.

A report does not contain your name, email address, photos, contacts, precise location or anything you have typed. The app takes no screenshots.

Like anything sent over the internet, a report arrives from an internet address. We do not store that address, or any location worked out from it.

We use these reports only to keep the app working. We do not use them for advertising, and we do not sell or share them.

Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., United States) processes this data on our behalf. Its privacy policy is at sentry.io/privacy.

Feedback you choose to send

"Send feedback" and "Report this hand" start an email to us. Nothing is sent unless you send the email. It contains:

  • whatever you write;
  • the app's version, your device model and your version of iOS;
  • the details of the game on the table, with the game itself attached as a file, so that we can look at the very cards you saw;
  • your email address and name, as with any email you send.

We use this only to answer you and to fix the problem you describe. We keep these emails for as long as they are useful for that, and delete them on request.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information for money.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, health or financial information.
  • We do not show full-screen, video or rewarded ads, and we do not show any ad before your first finished game.

Some privacy laws, such as California's, count letting advertising partners use a device identifier for personalised ads as "sharing" or "selling". That happens only if you allow tracking. You can refuse it, or withdraw it, with the Tracking setting described above, or avoid advertising altogether with the purchase that removes the ads.

Children

The app is a card game. It is not directed at children under 13, or under 16 in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app does not ask anyone for personal information.

Your choices and rights

  • You can refuse tracking, or withdraw it, in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • You can remove the ads, and with them all advertising data, with the purchase in the app.
  • You can stop all collection by deleting the app.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what is held about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Because crash reports are not connected to your identity, we usually cannot tell which reports came from you; if you have sent us feedback by email, we can find and delete that. For what an advertising partner holds, ask that partner, using its privacy policy linked above. Write to us at the address below and we will help.

Changes

If the app changes what it collects, this page will change first, and the date at the top will change with it.
